## What Is Generative AI?

Generative AI is an approach to artificial intelligence technology that allows the creation of various kinds of content, including text, pictures, music, videos, and computer code. Contrary to traditional AI technologies, which tend to concentrate their efforts on pattern recognition or prediction, generative AI creates something new on the basis of previously learned patterns.

Examples of the most famous generative AI include services such as ChatGPT (text generator), GitHub Copilot (assists with programming), and image generators, which produce images on the basis of the user's description.

## How Does Generative AI Work?

Like any machine learning model, generative AI algorithms use large data sets to learn patterns, which will allow them to provide proper answers to the user's requests during further interaction.

Generative AI algorithms may use [[neural-networks|neural networks]], as well as other approaches from [[machine-learning|machine learning]].

### Uses of Generative AI

There are multiple uses of generative AI:

1. Education: Assisting students in studying complex topics and developing creative ideas.

2. Programming: Assisting developers in coding and debugging.

3. Content creation: Generating images and various texts.

4. Productivity: Information summarization and organization of tasks.

## Benefits and Limitations

An advantage of generative AI is its ability to assist humans in accomplishing some actions more effectively. Nevertheless, the data created by the system is not necessarily accurate; thus, AI may provide erroneous or misleading answers.

The issues above relate to [[ai-ethics|AI Ethics]] that is the study of AI usage responsibly and privacy considerations.

Having an idea of what generative AI is is important due to the growing popularity of these systems in various fields.
