## What Are AI Tools?

AI tools are applications based on artificial intelligence technology that help users accomplish various tasks. They can be used for writing, coding, researching information, and making images. Many AI tools apply [[machine-learning|Machine Learning]] to find patterns and generate useful results depending on the user’s request.

The popularity of AI tools grows because they allow users to simplify their daily routine and increase productivity.

## Examples of AI Tools

There are many AI tools that are used by people for various purposes.

### ChatGPT

ChatGPT is a chatbot that uses [[generative-ai|Generative AI]] to answer various user questions and provide explanations about the requested topic.

### GitHub Copilot

GitHub Copilot is a programming assistant that suggests users codes, explains various concepts related to programming and helps to debug.

### AI Image Generators

AI image generators help users to generate images according to their description.

## How AI Tools Are Useful to Students

It is possible to use AI tools to support students' efforts in studying, research, or solving programming problems. To illustrate, AI can enable students to understand hard subjects or explain errors in code.

Nevertheless, students should learn to understand things by themselves without relying on answers generated by AI.

## Benefits and Drawbacks

Among the advantages of AI tools, there is an ability to save time and increase efficiency while doing certain activities. Moreover, users get some new ideas and can easily comprehend information.

The drawback of AI tools lies in the fact that the information provided by them might be inaccurate. Therefore, users should verify any important information before using it.

All these issues are related to [[ai-ethics|AI Ethics]].
