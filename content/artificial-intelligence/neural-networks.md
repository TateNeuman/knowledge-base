## What Is a Neural Network?

A neural network is a machine learning algorithm designed to work like the human brain processes data and information. Neural networks consist of neurons that link together to process patterns of data. Neural networks are widely used in artificial intelligence tasks like visual recognition, natural language processing, and forecasting.

Neural networks differ from other kinds of computer software in that they do not have to be programmed; instead, they learn through examples.
## How Does a Neural Network Function?

Neural networks are organized into layers to analyze information.

The Input Layer: Information is provided to the input layer in forms like images, text, or numbers.

The Hidden Layers: They analyze the information mathematically to create patterns.

The Output Layer: Provides the output in the form of predictions or results.

## Real-World Applications

Applications of neural networks include:

- Facial recognition and Image Recognition

- Voice recognition assistants

- Translation of languages

- Autonomous Driving Cars

- AI Chatbots and AI Content Creation


Neural networks are an integral part of [[machine-learning|Machine Learning]] and an essential part of [[generative-ai|Generative AI]].

### Advantages and Disadvantages

One of the advantages of using neural networks is the ability to find complicated patterns in large sets of information. The training of advanced neural networks can be very resource-intensive.

Another disadvantage of neural networks is the difficulty in understanding the logic used by the neural network in certain cases. It is connected to AI Ethics when the AI has consequences for the life of a person.

By studying neural networks, we understand the working principle of most AI systems.
