# Assignment 02: Analyzing an Organizing System

**Selected website:** [Liquipedia Rocket League](https://liquipedia.net/rocketleague/Main_Page)

## Introduction

Liquipedia is a public, community-maintained knowledge base that organizes information about competitive video games. Its Rocket League section provides information about professional players, teams, tournaments, and competitive results. The system helps fans, players, and researchers locate information about the Rocket League esports scene without having to search across multiple websites.

## Primary Resources

The primary resources are articles and structured records about players, teams, tournaments, and matches. For example, a player profile for Zen contains information about his competitive career, team affiliations, and tournament achievements. A team page for Team Vitality organizes information about its roster, history, and competitive results. Tournament pages provide resources such as participating teams, brackets, standings, and match scores.

These resources include both descriptive information and structured data. Player names, tournament dates, and team affiliations serve as metadata that helps distinguish resources and provides context for interpreting competitive results.

## Primary Interactions

Liquipedia supports searching, browsing, researching, comparing, and fact-checking. A user can search for a specific player or browse tournament pages to discover upcoming competitions and previous results. For example, someone following the Rocket League Championship Series (RLCS) can examine tournament brackets to understand how teams progressed through a competition.

Users can also compare team performances by examining tournament placements and match results. References and external links help users verify information against other sources. Community editing provides another interaction by allowing contributors to maintain and update information.

## Classification System

Liquipedia uses a hybrid classification system that combines hierarchical organization with category-based and faceted navigation. Its hierarchical structure organizes information into broad resource types, such as players, teams, and tournaments. Tournament information can be further organized by competitive series, season, and individual event. Attributes such as region and tournament tier provide additional dimensions for locating and distinguishing competitions.

This approach is effective because users have different information needs. A casual fan might browse a favorite team's page, while a researcher might examine tournaments from a particular region or season. However, the amount of information and specialized terminology can make navigation difficult for newcomers. The system is especially effective for experienced esports fans who already understand competitive formats and terminology.

## Relationships Between Resources

Relationships are represented through internal hyperlinks, categories, roster tables, and tournament brackets. For example, a player's name on a team roster links to the player's individual profile. Tournament results connect teams to specific competitions, while brackets represent relationships between matches and show how winners advance.

These connections allow users to move between related resources without repeatedly searching for information. Categories also group resources with shared characteristics, while references connect claims to external sources. Together, these relationships make Liquipedia more than a collection of individual articles. They create a connected knowledge base that supports exploration, research, and a deeper understanding of competitive Rocket League.
